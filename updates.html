A New Chapter: Leaving Roblox and Moving to Our New Site

Following up on our last update, we have made the difficult decision to officially step away from Roblox. The verification barriers have made it impossible for us to maintain and share our projects the way we want to, so instead of fighting a losing battle, we are taking our creativity elsewhere.

This isn't the end of Virality Games—it's just a complete shift in direction. We have built a brand new central hub where you can follow our journey, see what we are working on next, and stay connected with the community outside of the Roblox ecosystem.

🌐 Check out our brand new official website here: sites.google.com/view/virality-games

While it's sad to close the door on the platform where we started, we are incredibly excited about the freedom our new home gives us. Thank you all for the immense support during this transition. Pack your bags, because the adventure continues over on our new site! 🚀

🟡 Notice MARCH 2026 🎮Virality games
We Can No Longer Publish Games on Roblox

As of this month, we are no longer able to publish or update public experiences on Roblox. When attempting to publish, we are met with a verification wall requiring either a government-issued ID or a real-money purchase to proceed.

We believe this is a direct result of our Esponja Obby getting deleted around 3 months ago. At the time of its deletion, we still had enough accumulated playtime across our experiences to meet Roblox's publishing requirements — so we didn't notice the impact straight away. But 3 months later, that playtime window has expired, and without Esponja Obby still active and racking up hours, we no longer have enough playtime to qualify for public publishing without additional verification.

⚠️ This means we cannot push any updates, new releases, or bug fixes to any of our games until this is resolved — if it can be resolved at all.

We want to be transparent with you: Virality Games may be going on an indefinite break from Roblox, or in the worst case, shutting down entirely. We are exploring our options, but right now things are not looking great. Roblox's new publishing requirements have effectively locked us out with no easy way back in.

We are incredibly grateful for every single person who played our games, especially Esponja Obby. None of this is the outcome we wanted. We'll keep you posted if anything changes. 💛

🟡 Notice NOVEMBER 2025 🧽 Bob Esponja Obby
Bob Esponja Obby Has Been Taken Down

We've removed Bob Esponja Obby from Roblox. The game used copyrighted characters that we did not have permission to use, and upon receiving a copyright concern, we took it down immediately.

⚠️ We take intellectual property rights seriously. No game is worth keeping up if it uses characters or content we don't have rights to. This was the right call, full stop.

We apologise to anyone who enjoyed the game. We're committed to only building original experiences — or ones we have explicit permission to create — going forward. The lessons learned here directly shaped how we approach development today.
