Evervale RP
⛔ Taken Down — 2026

Evervale RP was our very first game ever, created by brokenhavenofiacal in August 2023 — the title that started it all. We were incredibly proud of it. However, in 2026, a malicious virus was discovered inside the game's codebase. Rather than leave it live and risk harming our players, we made the immediate decision to take it down entirely. 📖 Our Journey

The Real Story

Before there was a studio, there was one person — brokenhavenofiacal — with Roblox Studio, a lot of ambition, and zero quit in them. Here's how we went from a solo side project in 2023 to a named studio with two live games in 2026.

🌟 Our Mission

"To create Roblox experiences so fun and so shareable that players can't help but spread them — bringing joy to as many people as possible, one game at a time."

— brokenhavenofiacal, Founder
💡 Design Philosophy

We believe Roblox games deserve real craft: thoughtful art direction, tight gameplay, honest testing, and community listening. These aren't optional extras — they're our minimum standard for everything we ship.

AUGUST 2023 · THE BEGINNING
🌱 One Person, One Dream

brokenhavenofiacal opens Roblox Studio and builds from scratch. The very first game — Evervale RP, an open-world roleplay experience — launches in August 2023. No studio name, no team, no roadmap. Just one creator who loves Roblox and wants to make something real.

2023 – 2024 · THE QUIET YEARS
😴 Evervale Goes Silent

After the initial launch buzz, Evervale RP enters a long period of inactivity through 2023 and all of 2024. The game sits dormant — no updates, no new content. brokenhavenofiacal is still around, but the project is essentially on ice. A quiet chapter, but not the end.

AUGUST 2025 · THE COMEBACK
🔄 Evervale RP Returns

Nearly two years after its original launch, Evervale RP comes back to life in August 2025. Updates begin rolling out, the game starts improving, and players start discovering it. Word begins to spread — slowly at first, then faster. The game that was left for dead starts going viral.

2025 · THE NAME
⚡ Virality Games is Born

Riding the momentum of Evervale's revival, the solo project officially becomes a named studio: Virality Games. The name captures everything — making experiences so fun they spread on their own. Four community groups launch. What started as a hobby is now something with an identity.

NOVEMBER 2025 · SETBACK
⚠️ Bob Esponja Obby Taken Down

Shortly after Virality Games is officially named, a new obby game launches — but it uses characters from copyrighted IP without permission. Upon receiving a copyright concern, it's taken down immediately. A hard lesson learned early in the studio era: no shortcuts, no borrowed characters, ever.

2026 · BIG LAUNCHES
🛒🍕 Two Proper Games Ship

2026 is the year it all comes together. Super Gaming Cart Ride and Rainbow Pizza Obby both launch under the Virality Games banner — fully polished, community-ready, and built with the lessons of every game before them. Players respond enthusiastically. The studio has arrived.

2026 · SECOND SETBACK
⚠️ Evervale RP Taken Down

A malicious virus is discovered in the original Evervale RP codebase — our very first game, the one that started it all. Despite the emotional weight, it's taken down immediately to protect players. No hesitation. The door remains open for a clean rebuilt version someday.

NOW · 2026
🚀 Two Live Games, Four Groups, More Coming

Four games made. Two still standing. Four active community groups. A studio with a real name, a real history, and a clear mission. Virality Games is still growing — and the best is absolutely still ahead.

What is this?
Virality Games is an independent game studio that creates free games on Roblox — a popular online gaming platform. Their games include a cart ride adventure and a colourful obstacle course. Both are simple, lighthearted experiences aimed at all ages.
🆓
Does it cost money?
Both games are free to play on Roblox. We do offer optional in-game powerups purchasable with Robux — such as exclusive in-game gear — but these are purely cosmetic or bonus items. The full gameplay experience is always available without any spending.
🛡️
Is the content appropriate?
Yes. All Virality Games titles are designed to be wholesome, family-friendly, and appropriate for all ages. There is no violence, mature themes, or inappropriate content. Games are played within Roblox, which has its own moderation and safety systems.
⚠️
Why were some games removed?
Two games were taken down: one in 2025 due to copyrighted characters being used without permission (removed immediately upon notification), and one in 2026 after a security vulnerability was found in the code. Both were removed proactively to protect players — this shows the studio acts responsibly.
👥
What are the Roblox groups?
The studio has four free Roblox community groups where fans can connect and receive updates. Groups on Roblox are moderated by Roblox itself. Joining a group does not cost anything and does not require personal information.
📞
Who runs this?
Virality Games was founded by a Roblox developer known as brokenhavenofiacal in 2023. The studio is independent and operates entirely within the Roblox platform. If you have concerns, you can reach out via the main Roblox group.
