What Is Roblox Plus?

Roblox's brand new subscription is replacing Premium on April 30. Here's everything you need to know before it launches.

⏱️ 7 min read 🔴 Just announced 📅 Launches April 30, 2026
Home › Guides › What Is Roblox Plus
🔴 Breaking

Roblox officially announced Roblox Plus on April 10, 2026. It launches April 30. This page will be updated as more details are confirmed.

⚡ Short answer

Roblox Plus is a new $4.99/month subscription that replaces Roblox Premium. You get 10% off items (rising to 20% from month 3 onward), unlimited free private servers, free Robux transfers, and full marketplace access for trading and reselling limited items. Roblox stops selling Premium to new subscribers on April 30.

One tier, one price. Unlike the old Premium which had three price points ($4.99, $9.99, $19.99 with different Robux amounts baked in), Roblox Plus is a flat $4.99. A monthly Robux package will be added as a separate add-on starting in May 2026.

🏠 Free Private Servers

With Roblox Plus, every paid private server across the entire platform becomes free. You can jump into a friend's server or create your own without spending any Robux. Creators are still compensated when you spend meaningful time in their servers.

Creators earn when Plus subscribers spend 60 or more cumulative minutes in a paid private server over a 30-day period. The payout is based on the server's listed price, capped at 100 Robux per subscriber.

🎪 Marketplace Access & Limited Trading

Roblox Plus includes full access to the Limiteds marketplace — the same privileges that used to require a Premium subscription. You can trade limited items with other players, resell Limiteds you own, and publish your own avatar items for sale on the marketplace.

⚠️ New to trading Roblox Limiteds?

Trading limiteds sounds simple but there are traps that catch even experienced players out. One of the most common is accepting an item that looks valuable because of its RAP — but is actually worth a fraction of that number due to price manipulation. This is called a projected item, and it's one of the easiest ways to lose serious value in a trade.

🎮 Bonus: Earn Robux by Referring Players (Developers)

If you're a game developer, Roblox Plus has a creator-side referral system worth knowing about. When a player subscribes to Plus through your game using the PromptRobloxSubscriptionPurchase API, you earn a bonus of 250 Robux per month for their first three consecutive months — up to 750 Robux per subscriber you refer.

🚫 What Happens to Roblox Premium?

🚫 Premium is being discontinued

Roblox stops selling Premium to new subscribers on April 30, 2026 — the same day Plus launches. If you cancel your existing Premium after that date, you won't be able to resubscribe to it.

Existing Premium subscribers keep their benefits until May 30, 2026, after which some things change. Premium subscribers also get a 1-month free trial of Roblox Plus — Roblox will email a claim link on launch day.
