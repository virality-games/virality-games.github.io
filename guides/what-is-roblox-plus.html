What Is Roblox Plus?

Roblox's new subscription replaces Premium on April 30. Here's everything confirmed so far — including answers straight from Roblox staff.

⏱️ 7 min read 🔴 Announced Apr 10 📅 Launches Apr 30, 2026
Home › Guides › What Is Roblox Plus
🔴 Announced

Roblox officially announced Plus on April 10, 2026. This page was last updated April 11 with new confirmed details from Roblox staff.

📝 April 11 update: Added confirmed details on the Plus badge, limited item discounts, and Lifetime Builders Club / Premium grandfather situation — all confirmed directly by Roblox staff on the DevForum.
⚡ Short answer

Roblox Plus is a new $4.99/month subscription that replaces Premium. You get 10–20% off everything including limited items, a Plus badge next to your name, free unlimited private servers, free Robux transfers, and full marketplace access for trading and reselling limiteds. Roblox stops selling Premium on April 30.

💰 How Much Does It Cost?

Roblox Plus
$4.99

📅 Launches April 30, 2026

One tier, one price. Unlike the old Premium which had three price points ($4.99, $9.99, $19.99 with different Robux amounts baked in), Roblox Plus is a flat $4.99. A monthly Robux package will be added as a separate add-on starting in May 2026.

🤔 Worth noting

The $4.99 tier is cheaper than the $9.99 and $19.99 Premium tiers, but those came with monthly Robux. Plus currently doesn't include Robux at all until May — so if you were on a higher Premium tier mainly for the Robux stipend, you might actually be getting less value out of the gate.

🆕 What Do You Get?

🏷️

Discount on Everything

10% off in-game items, gamepasses, avatar items, developer subscriptions and more. Confirmed to also apply to limited items. The discount is paid by Roblox — creators still earn their full cut.

10% from day 1, 20% from month 3
🏠

Free Private Servers

Any paid private server on the platform becomes completely free for you. Creators still get paid when you spend 60+ cumulative minutes in their server per month.

Unlimited, across all games
🔁

Free Robux Transfers

Send Robux to friends at no cost. Normally transfers carry a fee — Plus removes it entirely. Players under 18 still need parental approval.

Zero transfer fees
🏭

Marketplace Access

Trade and resell limited items, publish your own avatar items for sale, and participate in the full Limiteds marketplace — everything Premium used to gate.

Trading + publishing included

📈 The Discount Gets Better Over Time

Your discount automatically doubles after your third consecutive month as a subscriber — no action needed. And yes, this applies to limited items too, confirmed directly by Roblox staff.

One confirmed exception: the discount does not apply to ads credit, UGC publishing fees, group fees, or items over 1 million Robux.

🎪 Confirmed: Discount Applies to Limited Items

This was one of the first things the Roblox trading community asked about. A Roblox staff member confirmed it directly on the DevForum:

guacandtoast Roblox Staff OP

Yes, the 20% discount also applies to limited items. Discounts do not apply to ads credit, UGC publishing fees, group fees (name or roles), or items over 1M Robux.

Yes, a subscription will be needed to trade and/or resell limiteds.

Confirmed by Roblox staff: the discount applies to limiteds, and you need Plus (or existing Premium) to trade them
🤔 Something to think about

If you're an active limited trader, this could actually save you real Robux on purchases — a 20% discount on a 100,000 Robux limited is 20,000 Robux off. But it also means anyone without a subscription will be locked out of trading entirely once Premium is phased out. That's a pretty significant paywall for a feature that used to come with the cheaper Premium tier.

⚠️ New to trading Roblox Limiteds?

Before you start spending Robux on limiteds, there's one trap that catches even experienced traders: items that look valuable because of their RAP but are actually worth a fraction of that number due to price manipulation. This is called a projected item, and with the new discount making limiteds more accessible, it's more important than ever to know what to look for.

🏆 The Plus Badge

Yes, Roblox Plus will have its own badge that shows next to your username — just like the old Premium diamond badge. Confirmed by Roblox staff.

guacandtoast Roblox Staff OP

Yes, all Plus subscribers will have a badge next to their name.

The Premium badge is retiring on May 30, 2026.

Plus badge confirmed — the old Premium diamond disappears May 30

🏠 Free Private Servers

With Roblox Plus, every paid private server across the entire platform becomes free to join. Creators are still compensated when Plus subscribers spend meaningful time in their servers.

Private servers show “Free with Plus”
The in-game Plus subscription prompt on mobile

Creators earn when Plus subscribers spend 60 or more cumulative minutes in a paid private server over 30 days. Payout is based on the server's price, capped at 100 Robux per subscriber.

Private server payout breakdown — 60 minutes minimum to qualify, capped at 100 Robux per subscriber

🏭 What About Lifetime Builders Club Users?

Old-school Roblox players who held Lifetime Builders Club and were grandfathered into Lifetime Premium have been asking what this means for them. Roblox staff addressed it:

guacandtoast Roblox Staff OP

Hi there, some of us on the team are Lifetime Builder Club members too!

You can still keep your Premium account if you like (with some of the benefit changes coming on May 30, 2026).

In addition, if you'd like to try out Plus, you'll receive a 1-month free trial link in your inbox on launch day, Apr 30, 2026. You can choose Plus in addition to or in place of Premium, the choice is all yours.

Lifetime BC users keep their Premium and get a free Plus trial on launch day
🤔 Fair question

Lifetime Builders Club was a product people paid significant money for in the early days of Roblox. The fact that the badge is going away and some benefits are changing — even for lifetime members — feels like a bit of a raw deal. Roblox acknowledged it at least, and letting them keep Premium is the right call, but it's understandable if longtime players feel a little stung by this one.

🛠 For Developers: Earn From Plus Sign-Ups

If you run a Roblox game, you can earn a referral bonus when players subscribe to Plus through your experience. Using the PromptRobloxSubscriptionPurchase API, you earn 250 Robux per month for the subscriber's first three consecutive months — up to 750 Robux per referred subscriber.

750 Robux total per subscriber you refer — paid over their first three months

🚫 What Happens to Roblox Premium?

🚫 Premium is being discontinued

Roblox stops selling Premium to new subscribers on April 30, 2026. If you cancel your existing Premium after that date, you won't be able to resubscribe. Some benefits change on May 30, 2026.

Existing Premium subscribers get a 1-month free trial of Roblox Plus via email on launch day.
