How to Get Admin in EVERY Roblox Game

6 methods that actually work — and method #5 is so obvious nobody ever talks about it.

⚡ Before you read
There is no magic script or cheat that gives you admin in literally every game — each game controls its own admin system. But there ARE methods that work almost every time, and most people never even try them. Keep reading.

✓ The 6 Methods That Actually Work

1

Make your own game ✓ 100% guaranteed

This is the only method that works in literally every single case — because it's your game. Full admin, total control, invite whoever you want, ban whoever you want. Nobody can take it away. Roblox Studio is free and you can have something published in under an hour even with zero experience.

How to Make Your First Roblox Game →
2

Just ask the owner 🔥 Underrated

This sounds way too simple. It works way more than it should. If the game owner is in the server, just send them a message. Small developers love players who actually talk to them — especially if you've been playing for a while or left a good review. The worst they can say is no.

3

Buy the admin gamepass ⭐ Most reliable

Most games with admin commands sell them as a gamepass — check the store tab before you even join. If there's an admin pass listed, that's the intended way to get it. Kohl's Admin, HD Admin, custom systems — they almost always have a purchasable version. Check the store first, save yourself the awkward ask.

4

Join the group and rank up ⌛ Takes time

Roleplay and group games often give admin commands based on rank. Join the official group, show up regularly, don't cause trouble. Stick around long enough and promotions happen. It's slow, it's free, and it lasts longer than any other method.

5

🤫 The one nobody talks about

Just play games that give everyone admin by default. Search Roblox for “free admin”, “admin commands”, or “admin abuse” — hundreds of games exist specifically so every player spawns in with full commands. Want to fly, kick people, spawn stuff, and go completely unhinged? These games were built for exactly that. No tricks, no purchases, just join and go.

6

Become a game tester 💼 Networking

Developers regularly give admin to people who help test games before release. Join developer Discord servers, offer genuine feedback, be helpful. It takes more effort than any other method but opens way more doors — and you get early access to games too.

If you just want to mess around with commands right now, search “free admin” or “admin commands” on Roblox. Hundreds of games give everyone full access the moment you spawn in.

🚨 These Will NOT Work (And Will Steal Your Account)

✗“Free admin scripts” you paste into chat — they either do nothing or hand your session token straight to a stranger.
✗Websites asking for your username to “give you admin” — they want your password. Every time.
✗“Working admin hack 2025” YouTube videos — the link in the description is malware or a fake Roblox login page.
✗Discord bots offering free admin — they want your .ROBLOSECURITY cookie, which gives full access to your account.
✗Typing “:fly” into the chat bar — chat commands only work if the game already gave you admin. Typing them into chat does absolutely nothing.
